Toyota Hilux spare parts are an important category for importers, distributors and repair workshops. Correct identification matters because generations, engines and regional specifications can affect fitment.

## Understanding Hilux model variations

Hilux Vigo is commonly associated with the seventh generation, while Hilux Revo is commonly associated with the eighth generation. Rocco and GR Sport are variant names used in selected markets. These names alone cannot establish interchangeability.

## Common replacement part categories

- **Engine:** oil and fuel filters, gaskets, water pumps and belts.
- **Suspension and steering:** shock absorbers, control arms, ball joints and tie rod ends.
- **Braking:** pads, discs, shoes and related components.
- **Body:** grilles, lamps, mirrors and exterior trim.

## Verify compatibility before ordering

Request the VIN or vehicle model code, production date, engine code, drivetrain configuration and original part number. Where relevant, compare photographs and measurements. Some OE references may have been superseded, so confirm the actual application before shipment.

## Tips for international wholesale buyers

Prepare a purchasing list with part numbers, quantities, product descriptions and destination country. Confirm whether quoted items are genuine, OEM-supplier or independent aftermarket products, and request appropriate packaging and shipment documentation.
